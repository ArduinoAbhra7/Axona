Axona™ - High-Performance AI Study Workspace

Supercharge your learning with an elite, integrated cognitive workspace. Designed specifically for students preparing for competitive exams like JEE, NEET, and board exams.

Core Features

Atlas™ - AI Planner

Generate highly customized, time-blocked daily and weekly schedules. Atlas optimizes your time based on your weak topics and study goals.

NeuroLog™ - Study Diary

Log your daily study sessions, track topics covered, and sync progress automatically to your personal cloud-connected analytics dashboard.

Momentum™ - Study Tracker

Track your focus metrics, streak history, and total hours studied with rich data visualizations. Stay accountable and build long-term habits.

Recall™ - AI Revision

Generate AI-powered active recall cards and summary notes. Turn your weaknesses into flashcard decks automatically.

Oracle™ - Doubt Solver

Resolve your doubts instantly with step-by-step mathematical and conceptual derivations powered by high-speed Groq AI.

About Axona™

Axona is designed to solve the cognitive load issues faced by high-school and college prep students. By integrating 3D spatial environments, deep focus timers with binaural audio, and advanced AI agents, Axona delivers a premium environment for academic success. Developed by Abhranil Sen to empower scholars worldwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Axona cloud-connected?

Yes, all study stats, diary entries, and custom syllabus trackers are fully synced to Google Firestore, enabling cloud access across all your devices.

How does the AI Doubt Solver (Oracle™) work?

Oracle uses state-of-the-art LLMs via Groq Cloud to provide extremely fast, detailed explanations, derivations, and hints for complex math and science questions.
